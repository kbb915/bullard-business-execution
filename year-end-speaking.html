Holiday & Year-End Speaking

A meaningful way to close the year together.

Four talk options for organizations that want their holiday party, employee appreciation event, or year-end gathering to leave people feeling seen, grateful, and ready for what comes next.

End the Year Well

The right message can help people recognize what the year has meant.

Each talk can be tailored to your organization, audience, and event. The tone is practical, warm, and engaging—designed to create reflection without turning a celebration into another work session.

Four Talk Options

Choose the message your team needs most.

These options work as keynotes or shorter featured talks for company celebrations, leadership gatherings, and appreciation events.

01

You've Done More Than You Think

Giving Yourself Credit for the Year You've Had

We tend to remember what went wrong, what we didn't finish, and what we wish we'd done differently. Meanwhile, we overlook what we've accomplished, endured, learned, and contributed.

Audience TakeawayI have more to appreciate about this year than I realized.
02

Don't Take Everything Into the New Year

What to Keep, What to Change, and What to Leave Behind

A different year doesn't guarantee a different experience. We have to examine what we're choosing to continue and make one intentional choice about how we'll approach what comes next.

Audience TakeawayI can make different choices regardless of what happens around me.
03

The People Make the Difference

Appreciating the People Who Make Our Work—and Our Lives—Better

Our accomplishments matter, but so do the people who helped us achieve them and the way we treated people along the way. Appreciation can extend well beyond the holiday season.

Audience TakeawayI have an opportunity to make someone's experience better, regardless of my position.
04

Designed Around Your Organization

A Customized Talk for What Your Team Needs Now

Every organization enters year-end with its own successes, challenges, and priorities. Kevin can create a talk around the message your team needs, shaped to fit your culture, audience, and event.
