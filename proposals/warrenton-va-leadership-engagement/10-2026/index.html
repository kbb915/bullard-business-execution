01The Opportunity

Give the vision a team that can carry it.

A 12-month engagement to strengthen Jud’s core leadership team and turn the offsite’s shared language into a durable way of working.

The one-day offsite creates a starting point: the Senior Leadership Team learns to recognize limiting stories, return to commitments, and apply that shift to the Town’s real work.

The next opportunity is to make sure the practice survives the return to normal workloads. Jud should not have to carry the Town’s 12-month vision alone. A small group of trusted leaders can help translate it into decisions, conversations, and coordinated action across the SLT.

From a strong day to a stronger leadership system.

The engagement develops Jud and three leaders who are especially instrumental to getting things done, then uses quarterly SLT sessions to spread the practice and reconnect it to Town priorities.

03Coaching Rhythm

Develop the four leaders closest to execution.

Consistent individual coaching turns insight into leadership behavior and keeps the Town’s real work at the center.

Town Manager coaching

Jud · twice monthly

  • Prepare for consequential decisions and conversations.
  • Translate the 12-month vision into leadership priorities.
  • Strengthen delegation, alignment, and accountability.
  • Recognize limiting stories and lead from commitment.
Core leader coaching

Three leaders · twice monthly each

  • Build enterprise leadership beyond functional roles.
  • Strengthen ownership and cross-team influence.
  • Work through current barriers and priorities.
  • Help Jud and the SLT sustain agreed practices.
Coaching follows the work.

Sessions will use live decisions, relationships, and Town priorities. The goal is visible progress in how leaders operate, not completion of a disconnected curriculum.

04SLT Reinforcement

Reconnect the full team every quarter.

Quarterly sessions help the 12-person SLT practice the shared language, address current work, and see how the Town’s priorities fit together.

01Reinforce

Return to commitments

Revisit the language introduced at the offsite, surface limiting stories, and reset conversations around the outcomes the team wants.

02Apply

Work on real Town priorities

Use the budget, the 12-month plan, emerging issues, and cross-department needs as the material for stronger leadership practice.

03Align

Clarify ownership and coordination

Sessions may be held virtually to reduce cost and travel while maintaining a reliable quarterly cadence.

0512-Month Roadmap

A consistent cadence built around real work.

The offsite launches the language. Coaching and quarterly reinforcement keep it alive through the year.

LaunchOffsite

Establish shared language

Introduce limiting stories and commitments, practice with real scenarios, and identify the three leaders who will help Jud carry the work forward.

MonthlyMonths 1–12

Coach the core four

Hold twice-monthly individual coaching with Jud and each of his three selected leaders, using current leadership challenges and Town priorities.

QuarterlyFour sessions

Strengthen the SLT

Bring the full team together to reinforce commitments, address cross-department work, and reconnect the leadership practice to the 12-month vision.

Year endSustain

Capture progress and next practices

Assess what changed, identify what still needs attention, and define the practices the Town can continue without outside facilitation.

07Scope & Next Step

Confirm the leaders. Begin the year.

The final scope will follow selection of the three core leaders and confirmation of the coaching and quarterly-session cadence.

Included in the 12-month engagement

Twice-monthly individual coaching for Jud; twice-monthly individual coaching for each of three leaders chosen by Jud; four quarterly SLT sessions; preparation and coordination; and a year-end sustainability review.

Delivery

Individual coaching and quarterly SLT sessions may be virtual. Any requested onsite work or travel beyond the one-day offsite will be agreed separately.
