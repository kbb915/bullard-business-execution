Chad Brumit Proposal
02 / The Shift

From protecting the status quo to building the standard.

Autonomy

Leaders proactively tackle what is in front of them, make decisions, and learn from mistakes without waiting for permission.

Candor

Feedback happens in real time. Quarterly conversations formalize the last 90 days; they do not introduce problems for the first time.

High standards

Strong performance is named, developed, and multiplied—without confusing kindness with avoiding accountability.

“The worst thing that happens is when they leave, they know what true leadership and excellent leadership looks like. Best thing that happens is they love it, and they become lifetime team members, and they help us grow that culture long-term.”
— Chad Brumit
