Chad Brumit Proposal
03 / The Engagement

One day a month. Real work between sessions.

Over six months, each leader receives six two-hour in-person leadership labs, six 45-minute individual coaching sessions, three onsite coaching sessions, three virtual coaching sessions, and a brief mid-month accountability/application touchpoint.

Leadership lab

Six two-hour in-person leadership labs with the cohort, grounded in current CoVerica situations.

Individual coaching

Each leader receives one 45-minute coaching session per month, alternating between onsite and virtual.

Applied fieldwork

Each month, leaders apply the session’s ideas to a live leadership challenge, then return with evidence of what they tried, what changed, and where they need to adjust.

Progress check

A brief individual touchpoint to reinforce application, address obstacles, and maintain momentum.
