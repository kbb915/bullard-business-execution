A focused leadership-development engagement

Give CoVerica's next leaders room to lead.

A practical six-month development experience for the leaders at the center of CoVerica's next chapter—building confidence, candor, accountability, and the habits that protect culture while the business grows.

What I heard
You are building the core leadership nucleus for two connected organizations—and you want them equipped to move from “tight leash” to confident, proactive ownership.

The goal is not leadership training in the abstract. It is helping good, young leaders do their actual work better: lead teams, manage up, work across functions, serve clients, and handle hard conversations before small issues become quarterly surprises.

That fits CoVerica's emphasis on personalized service, transparent communication, and future-ready growth.

The shift

From protecting the status quo to building the standard.

Autonomy

Leaders proactively tackle what is in front of them, make decisions, and learn from mistakes without waiting for permission.

Candor

Feedback happens in real time. Quarterly conversations formalize the last 90 days; they do not introduce problems for the first time.

High standards

Strong performance is named, developed, and multiplied—without confusing kindness with avoiding accountability.

“The worst thing that happens is when they leave, they know what true leadership and excellent leadership looks like. Best thing that happens is they love it, and they become lifetime team members, and they help us grow that culture long-term.”
The engagement

One day a month. Real work between sessions.

A deliberately light-touch cadence that respects a team already “drinking from a fire hose,” while making development visible in the work they are already doing.

01 / MONTHLY SESSION

Leadership lab

A two-hour group session with the core cohort, grounded in current CoVerica situations.

02 / SAME DAY

Individual coaching

One-on-one conversations to translate the group work into each leader's role and decisions.

03 / BETWEEN SESSIONS

Applied homework

Specific experiments tied to live work, not random leadership assignments.

04 / MID-MONTH

Progress check-in

A brief touchpoint to reinforce application, remove friction, and keep momentum.

What we will build

A common language for excellent leadership.

  • Anticipating issues instead of waiting for quarterly reviews
  • Leaning into hard conversations with clarity and respect
  • Giving and receiving challenge without defensiveness
  • Turning high standards into coaching and development
  • Making mistakes safe to surface, and useful to learn from
  • Leading across the two organizations with shared expectations
Recommended starting point

Six months to establish the habits, language, and confidence that can scale.

Start with the initial core cohort of approximately six to seven leaders across CoVerica and CoVerica Agency Alliance. Reevaluate together at the end of six months and decide what should continue, expand, or change.
