A focused leadership-development engagement

Give CoVerica's next leaders room to lead.

A practical six-month development experience for the leaders at the center of CoVerica's next chapter—building confidence, candor, accountability, and the habits that protect culture while the business grows.

What I heard
You are building the core leadership nucleus for two connected organizations—and you want them equipped to move from “tight leash” to confident, proactive ownership.

The goal is not leadership training in the abstract. It is helping good, young leaders do their actual work better: lead teams, manage up, work across functions, serve clients, and handle hard conversations before small issues become quarterly surprises.

That fits CoVerica's emphasis on personalized service, transparent communication, and future-ready growth.

The shift

From protecting the status quo to building the standard.

Autonomy

Leaders proactively tackle what is in front of them, make decisions, and learn from mistakes without waiting for permission.

Candor

Feedback happens in real time. Quarterly conversations formalize the last 90 days; they do not introduce problems for the first time.

High standards

Strong performance is named, developed, and multiplied—without confusing kindness with avoiding accountability.

“The worst thing that happens is when they leave, they know what true leadership and excellent leadership looks like. Best thing that happens is they love it, and they become lifetime team members, and they help us grow that culture long-term.”
— Chad Brumit
The engagement

One day a month. Real work between sessions.

A deliberately light-touch cadence that respects a team already “drinking from a fire hose,” while making development visible in the work they are already doing.

Over six months, each leader receives: six two-hour in-person leadership labs, six 45-minute individual coaching sessions, three onsite coaching sessions, three virtual coaching sessions, and a brief mid-month accountability/application touchpoint.

01 / MONTHLY SESSION

Leadership lab

Six two-hour in-person leadership labs with the cohort, grounded in current CoVerica situations.

02 / INDIVIDUAL COACHING

Onsite + virtual

Each leader receives one 45-minute coaching session per month. Over six months, sessions alternate between onsite and virtual, giving each leader three in-person and three virtual coaching conversations.

03 / BETWEEN SESSIONS

Applied fieldwork

Each month, leaders apply the session’s ideas to a live leadership challenge, then return with evidence of what they tried, what changed, and where they need to adjust.

04 / MID-MONTH

Progress check

A brief individual touchpoint to reinforce application, address obstacles, and maintain momentum between monthly sessions.

Confidential coaching conversations remain confidential. Your debrief can focus on themes, organizational barriers, progress against leadership objectives, and reinforcement priorities.

For six leaders, three leaders may be coached onsite each month and three virtually. For seven leaders, the onsite group can rotate between three and four each month so everyone receives exactly three onsite and three virtual sessions across the engagement.

What we will build

A common language for excellent leadership.

  • Anticipating issues instead of waiting for quarterly reviews
  • Leaning into hard conversations with clarity and respect
  • Giving and receiving challenge without defensiveness
  • Turning high standards into coaching and development
  • Making mistakes safe to surface, and useful to learn from
  • Leading across the two organizations with shared expectations
Recommended starting point

Six months to establish the habits, language, and confidence that can scale.

Start with the initial core cohort of approximately six to seven leaders at CoVerica. Reevaluate together at the end of six months and decide what should continue, expand, or change.

Success will look like

Leaders who do not wait for permission to lead.

By the end of the initial term, the cohort should have stronger confidence to act, clearer standards for performance, more direct conversations, and a repeatable way to turn leadership principles into day-to-day behavior.

Most importantly, I don't want you to be the only person carrying the culture. I want the leaders being developed to be able to carry it forward themselves.
