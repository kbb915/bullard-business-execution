One City leadership team.

The Opportunity

San Angelo has capable department heads. The opportunity is to help them operate less as separate departments and more as one City leadership team—communicating directly, anticipating shared challenges, and coordinating around priorities that cross organizational boundaries.

The central shift

Move from information sharing to enterprise leadership.

Use real City priorities as the work through which stronger leadership practices are built.

01 / LAUNCH

Build common language

Bring all directors together onsite for 1.5–2 days.

02 / WORK

Activate the SLT

Use monthly sessions to address real enterprise-level issues.

03 / REINFORCE

Sustain the change

Combine quarterly director sessions with City Manager coaching.

Build common language.
Connect it to City work.

A 1.5–2 day onsite launch brings all 25–30 directors together to strengthen relationships, understand how people work, and establish expectations for leading across departments.

01

Understand how people contribute

Use Working Genius to identify how leaders naturally contribute, where frustration occurs, and how differences affect teamwork and execution.

02

Reduce silo behavior

Examine where departmental boundaries slow decisions, weaken communication, or prevent leaders from involving the right people early enough.

03

Establish leadership commitments

Agree on observable behaviors for direct communication, cross-department coordination, shared accountability, and support of the strategic plan.

Launch focus

  • Working Genius and team contribution.
  • Cross-department relationships.

  • Leadership behavior tied to City priorities.

Launch outputs

  • Clear leadership commitments.
  • Priority enterprise issues for the SLT.
  • A baseline for measuring progress.

From retreat to operating rhythm

The launch begins the work. Monthly and quarterly reinforcement keeps insights from becoming another set of retreat notes that fade under normal workloads.

03 / Senior Leadership Team

Put the existing SLT
to work on the City.

The City’s existing cross-functional Senior Leadership Team becomes the primary working group for enterprise-level issues that no single department can solve alone.

~10Existing cross-functional members
MonthlyVirtual working sessions
2× monthlyCity Manager coaching

Purpose

The SLT already includes leaders from functions such as IT, economic development, finance, and the Assistant City Manager’s office. The engagement strengthens this group’s ability to anticipate challenges, frame enterprise decisions, and move work across departmental lines.

Monthly SLT work

  • Look six months ahead for emerging issues.
  • Address barriers before they become emergencies.
  • Clarify ownership and decision rights.
  • Coordinate initiatives tied to the strategic plan.
  • Reinforce agreed leadership practices.

Inclusive by design

  • Bring in additional directors when their expertise is needed.
  • Communicate decisions and context clearly.
  • Avoid creating an “inside group” perception.
  • Keep enterprise work connected to the full director team.

The full leadership group participates in the change. The smaller team helps lead it.

This design uses the same principle as a guiding coalition: a credible cross-functional team carries change forward through real organizational work.

04 / Engagement Rhythm

A consistent cadence.
Built around real work.

The engagement combines monthly enterprise work, quarterly reinforcement, and regular City Manager coaching rather than relying on a fixed curriculum of disconnected topics.

01

Monthly SLT work sessions / virtual

Facilitated conversations about what is coming, where coordination is needed, what decisions are slowing down, and which leaders need to be involved. Benefits, compensation, technology, and strategic initiatives become leadership work before they become problems.

02

City Manager coaching / twice monthly

Two 45-minute conversations each month help Phil reinforce the work from the top, prepare for difficult leadership moments, and connect SLT progress to broader organizational priorities.

03

Quarterly director sessions

Use the City’s existing director meeting where practical to reconnect the full group, reinforce commitments, address current cross-department needs, and share progress without adding unnecessary retreats.

04

Year-end sustainability brief

Assess what has changed, what still needs work, and which leadership practices the City should continue without outside facilitation.

Strategic plan integration

As the strategic plan takes shape, sessions will increasingly use actual City priorities as the work. Leadership development and City operations become one effort: helping the leadership team execute the plan together.

05 / Working Genius

A shared language
for real work.

Working Genius provides a simple shared language for understanding how leaders naturally contribute to work, where frustration occurs, and how teams can use the right people at the right stages of execution.

The role of the assessment

Make the work easier to see, discuss, and improve.

The assessment supports the engagement. It is not the engagement itself.

01 / CONTRIBUTE

See natural strengths

Understand where each leader brings energy and value to the work process.

02 / NOTICE

Name frustration

Identify work that drains energy, creates friction, or routinely stalls.

03 / APPLY

Involve the right people

Use the team’s capabilities more intentionally in meetings, projects, and decisions.

Applied in the launch

  • Individual profiles and team map.
  • Discussion of contributions and gaps.
  • Implications for communication and delegation.
  • Connections to current City work.

Applied throughout the year

  • Meeting design and participation.
  • Project staffing and handoffs.
  • Cross-department collaboration.
  • Decision-making and follow-through.

Design principle

Leadership practices will be coached inside real decisions, initiatives, and cross-department challenges—not taught as a sequence of monthly classroom topics.
