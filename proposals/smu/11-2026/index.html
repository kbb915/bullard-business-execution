01 / Purpose

Build confidence through practical application.

Provide Meredith with individualized coaching designed to strengthen her professional effectiveness, build confidence, and support her continued development and success at SMU.

Coaching will help Meredith think through workplace situations, take ownership of her professional growth, and apply practical strategies to her daily responsibilities.

The work will begin with clear development goals and remain grounded in the situations Meredith is navigating in her role. Each conversation will connect reflection to an action she can practice between sessions.

Develop stronger judgment, clearer communication, and greater ownership in the work Meredith is already doing.

02 / Proposed Areas of Focus

Three proposed foundations for professional effectiveness.

These proposed areas provide a starting point for the engagement. The final areas of focus will be established with Annette during the initial sponsor meeting.

  1. 01

    Ownership and Accountability

    Taking initiative, managing responsibilities, and following through on commitments.

  2. 02

    Communication and Feedback

    Communicating effectively, receiving and applying feedback, and clarifying expectations.

  3. 03

    Independent Thinking and Problem-Solving

Designed to adjust with the work. The areas of focus will be solidified during the initial meeting with Annette, then adjusted throughout the engagement based on Annette’s feedback and observations and Meredith’s progress.

04 / Sponsor Partnership

Clear alignment with Annette. Confidential coaching for Meredith.

Three sponsor touchpoints will keep the engagement connected to its purpose without compromising the confidentiality of Meredith’s coaching sessions.

  1. 01

    Pre-engagement meeting

    Kevin and Annette align on the purpose of coaching, organizational context, mutually agreed development goals, and communication boundaries.

  2. 02

    90-day check-in

    Kevin and Annette review progress toward the agreed goals and adjust coaching priorities as needed.

  3. 03

    Post-engagement review

    Kevin and Annette review overall progress, discuss sustainment, and identify appropriate next steps.

Confidentiality

Meredith’s individual coaching sessions will remain confidential, subject to agreed-upon exceptions. Sponsor meetings will focus on mutually agreed development goals, observable progress, and next steps—not the content of Meredith’s coaching conversations.
