01Purpose

A day to build shared commitment.

One day together. Real work in front of the team. A shared way to move it forward.

Jud, I propose a one-day offsite to help your senior leadership team recognize limiting stories, shift toward commitments, and practice that shift in the work already in front of them.

The day will connect leadership development with your budget discussions, 90-day assessment, and priorities for the next 12 months.
