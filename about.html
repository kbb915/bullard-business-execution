About Kevin Bullard

Practical perspective for leaders doing consequential work.

Kevin Bullard is an executive advisor, organizational effectiveness consultant, and former Chief Leadership Officer.

Experience

More than 20 years helping organizations work more effectively.

Kevin has worked across business, government, nonprofit, education, and other sectors in executive coaching, leadership effectiveness, organizational change, and execution.

  • Former Chief Leadership Officer for Garland, Texas, a municipality with approximately 2,400 employees.
  • Executive advisor and coach to leaders responsible for people, priorities, and difficult decisions.
  • Organizational effectiveness consultant focused on how leadership behavior, ownership, and execution interact.
About BBE

What guides the way we work.

Our vision, mission, and values shape how we see challenges, work with leaders, and help important ideas move into action.

Vision and Mission

Vision

A world where leaders and organizations see beyond their blind spots, turning bold ideas into extraordinary results.

Mission

Through tailored consulting, executive coaching, leadership programs, and speaking engagements, we help leaders and their teams uncover blind spots, shift their perspective, and take decisive action on their most important goals.

Core Values

Look

We put certainty to the test through curiosity and exploration. We ask the right questions to uncover blind spots and see the full picture.

Influence

We actively shape what happens around us rather than simply reacting to it. We step out of old patterns and intentionally create the environment we want to lead.

Contribute

We bring our full voice, effort, and presence to what matters most, showing up fully committed to our clients' breakthroughs.

Move Forward

We believe insight must lead to action. We focus on clear, decisive steps that create visible, meaningful change.

Work together
